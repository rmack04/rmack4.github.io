Introduction

These are some of the websites that helped me understand web servers and how they work. I used my research from Assignment 1 to help create this website. These resources explain web server history, HTTP communication, security, and other important information about web servers.

What I Learned

From researching web servers, I learned that they do more than just display websites. They also help manage traffic, protect information, and communicate with other applications. Learning about Apache and Nginx helped me understand why web servers are important to the internet.
