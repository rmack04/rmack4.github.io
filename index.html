Introduction to Web3

Web3 is an idea for a new generation of the internet that uses blockchain technology and decentralized networks. Unlike traditional websites, Web3 applications can allow users to interact without depending on one central company.

Key Terms

  • Blockchain
  • Cryptocurrency
  • Decentralization
  • Smart Contracts
  • Digital Wallets

What Is Web3?

Web3 focuses on giving users more control over digital assets and online interactions. Blockchain networks can keep shared records without one organization controlling the entire system.

Web1, Web2, and Web3

Generation Description
Web1 Mostly static websites used for reading information.
Web2 Interactive websites, social media, and user-generated content.
Web3 Decentralized applications, blockchain, and digital assets.

How Web3 Works

Web3 applications can use blockchain networks to record transactions and smart contracts to perform actions automatically when conditions are met.

  1. A user connects a digital wallet.
  2. The user interacts with an application.
  3. A transaction may be submitted to a blockchain.
  4. The network validates the transaction.
  5. The blockchain records the result.

Not every Web3 interaction requires a blockchain transaction.

Important Web3 Concepts

1. Blockchain

A shared digital ledger that records information across a network.

2. Decentralization

Distributing control across different participants instead of one authority.

3. Cryptocurrency

Digital assets that use cryptographic technology and blockchain networks.

4. Smart Contracts

Programs stored on a blockchain that execute predefined instructions.

5. Digital Wallets

Applications or devices used to manage keys and interact with blockchain networks.

6. Decentralized Applications

Applications that use decentralized networks or smart contracts.

7. NFTs

Unique blockchain tokens that can represent digital assets or rights.
